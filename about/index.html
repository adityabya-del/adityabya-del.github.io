About ClearBerth

ClearBerth is an independent app that makes an Indian train journey easier to follow, from the first waitlist check to the last station.

What ClearBerth does

ClearBerth is an app for people who travel on Indian Railways. It shows PNR status, draws your berth on a coach diagram, tracks where your train is and how late it runs, sends alerts when a waitlist number changes, and lets you search seat availability. The same app also has food and parcel tabs.

Why it exists

I built ClearBerth because I was the person refreshing my PNR every few minutes and doing delay maths in my head. I wanted one calm screen that answers the two questions that matter: where do I sit, and when does the train reach me.

It is not the official IRCTC app

ClearBerth is an independent app made for travellers. It is not run by IRCTC or Indian Railways. Use it alongside IRCTC for the things only IRCTC does. Our guides explain IRCTC’s own rules and link to the IRCTC page each one comes from.

What it costs

PNR status, the berth diagram and live train tracking are free and supported by short ads. If you want alerts, you pay once per journey. The price is shown in the app before you buy. There is no subscription.

About the train data

Train data comes from railway data providers. Live running data can lag or change, so treat times as best estimates and follow station announcements for anything critical. The train, route and station pages on this site show scheduled timetable figures. Real running times change with delays.

Languages

ClearBerth works in English and Hindi, including alerts. This site has Hindi pages for PNR status and for each train page.
