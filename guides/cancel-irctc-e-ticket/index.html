How to cancel an IRCTC e-ticket: charges, TDR and refund timing

What it costs to cancel an e-ticket on IRCTC, how the charge changes as departure gets closer, and what to do once the chart is prepared.

The short answer

A confirmed IRCTC e-ticket can be cancelled online until the chart is prepared, not at a counter. The charge depends on how long before departure you cancel. After the chart, you file a TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) online instead.

Charges for a confirmed ticket

IRCTC’s page lists these charges per passenger.

When you cancelCharge
More than 48 hours before departureFlat amount by class: AC First/Executive Rs.240, AC 2 Tier/First Class Rs.200, AC 3 Tier/AC Chair Car/AC 3 Economy Rs.180, Sleeper Rs.120, Second Class Rs.60
Within 48 hours and up to 12 hours before departure25% of the fare, subject to the flat minimum above
Less than 12 hours and up to 4 hours before departure (or until chart preparation, if earlier)50% of the fare, subject to minimum charges

For trains that depart before noon, the chart is usually prepared the previous night.

After the chart is prepared

  • A normal user cannot cancel online after chart preparation and must file a TDR online.
  • A confirmed ticket gets no refund if it is neither cancelled nor has a TDR filed online up to 4 hours before departure.
  • An RAC e-ticket gets no refund if it is not cancelled, or the TDR is not filed online, up to 30 minutes before departure.
  • Agents cannot cancel after chart preparation. They email etickets@irctc.co.in with the PNR, transaction ID and passenger details, and IRCTC files the TDR.

Party and family tickets

If some passengers on a ticket are confirmed and others are on RAC or the waitlist, the confirmed passengers still get a full refund minus clerkage. This needs cancelling, or filing TDRs online, for all passengers up to 30 minutes before departure.

Tatkal tickets

There is no refund on a confirmed Tatkal e-ticket. Cancelling a contingent or waitlisted Tatkal ticket carries charges under Railway rules. Partial cancellation is allowed.

If the train is cancelled

If the train is marked CANCELLED in the reservation system because of breaches, floods or accidents, a full refund is allowed when the ticket is cancelled within three days of the scheduled departure.

Waitlisted tickets

The IRCTC cancellation page gives no separate refund rule for ordinary waitlisted tickets. What happens to a ticket that is still waitlisted at charting is on another IRCTC page, summarised in our waitlist guide.

A note on dates

The IRCTC page shows no date, so the effective date of these charges cannot be confirmed from it. Check IRCTC for the current figures before you cancel.
