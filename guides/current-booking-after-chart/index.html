Current booking: buying a vacant berth after the chart is made

After the reservation chart is prepared, berths that are still empty can be booked as "current booking". This guide sets out IRCTC's rules.

Last checked: 27 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

What current booking is

IRCTC describes current booking as booking against vacant accommodation after charting, within the booking time limit set by Railways.

IRCTC's rules

  • All users, normal and agents, can book.
  • Only e-tickets can be booked.
  • Only confirmed tickets are booked.
  • Only the senior citizen and disabled concessions are allowed. (The senior citizen concession itself was withdrawn by Railways from 20 March 2020; see our senior citizen guide.)
  • The boarding point cannot be changed.
  • The name, age and gender cannot be changed.
  • In Premium and Suvidha trains, booking is allowed at the last booking fare.

When the chart is prepared

The Railway Board's booking page says reservation charts are normally prepared 4 hours before the departure of the train from its originating station. A Ministry of Railways release of 24 March 2026 says charts now happen 9 to 18 hours before departure instead of 4 hours. Current booking starts after the chart.

IRCTC's own trains

For the trains IRCTC runs itself, IRCTC says current booking opens after the first chart and stays open until 5 minutes before the scheduled departure.

Where to see empty berths

Current booking only works if a berth is still empty after the chart. ClearBerth's Book tab shows seats class by class for a date; tickets are booked on IRCTC.

In short

  • After charting, empty berths can be bought on IRCTC as confirmed e-tickets.
  • No boarding point, name, age or gender change on these tickets.
