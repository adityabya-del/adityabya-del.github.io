Railway concession for persons with disabilities (Divyangjan)

Indian Railways gives fare concessions to four categories of persons with disabilities, and keeps berths for them. This guide sets out the rules from the Ministry of Railways and IRCTC.

Last checked: 19 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

The concession continues

In a Lok Sabha reply on 11 March 2026 the Ministry of Railways said concessions continue for 4 categories of persons with disabilities (Divyangjans).

Who qualifies and how much

A Ministry of Railways release of 3 December 2019 sets out the four categories and the concession on single journey tickets:

CategoryConcession
Orthopedically handicapped or paraplegic persons who cannot travel without an escort75% in Second, Sleeper, First Class, AC Chair Car and AC 3-tier; 50% in AC 2-tier and AC First; 25% in 3AC and AC Chair Car of Rajdhani/Shatabdi. One escort gets the same concession.
Mentally challenged persons who cannot travel without an escortSame as above
Persons with visual impairment with total absence of sight, travelling alone or with an escortSame as above
Persons with total hearing and speech impairment (both in the same person), travelling alone or with an escort50% in Second, Sleeper and First Class. One escort gets the same concession.
These rates are from the 2019 release. Check the fare shown on IRCTC when you book.

The Railway photo ID card

To book a concessional ticket online, the passenger needs the photo identity card issued by Railways for persons with disabilities. A Southern Railway release says these ID cards can be applied for and renewed online at divyangjanid.indianrail.gov.in.

Booking on IRCTC

IRCTC says the booking form asks for the details on the Railway photo ID card: name, age, gender, concession type, ID card number, date of birth and the "valid up to" date. The passenger must carry the card for checking on and off the train, and an escort must carry the photo ID given at booking. Passengers can be saved in the IRCTC Master List.

Reserved berths

In the March 2026 reply, the Ministry said a quota for persons with disabilities and their attendants is kept in mail/express trains, including Rajdhani and Shatabdi type trains:

  • Four berths in Sleeper (two lower and two middle)
  • Four berths in 3AC/3E (two lower and two middle)
  • Four seats in Second Sitting (2S) or AC Chair Car (CC), where the train has more than two coaches of that class

Persons with disabilities are also allotted lower berths automatically, subject to availability, and get priority for lower berths that fall vacant during the journey.

In short

  • Four categories qualify; the escort gets the same concession.
  • Book online with the Railway photo ID card and carry it on the train.
  • Four berths per class are kept in mail/express trains.
