Foreign Tourist quota on IRCTC: booking, fare and passport rules

Foreign tourists and NRIs with a valid passport can book train tickets online on IRCTC under the Foreign Tourist (FT) quota. These are IRCTC's rules for it.

Last checked: 19 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

Who can use it

IRCTC says Foreign Tourists, including NRIs, holding a valid passport can book under the Foreign Tourist quota on the IRCTC website. Users from outside India register as international users and pay a registration fee of ₹100 + GST. They must verify an international mobile number in their IRCTC profile with an OTP.

How far ahead

FT quota tickets can be booked up to 365 days in advance. If you book within the normal Advance Reservation Period (60 days), the berth is allotted at booking; if you book earlier, the berth is allotted when the period opens, and you are told by international SMS. If no berth is allotted, the ticket is cancelled automatically.

Classes and fare

  • Classes: Executive (EC), 1st AC, 2nd AC, 3rd AC, AC Chair Car, Sleeper and Second Sitting, in all trains including Shatabdi, Rajdhani, Duronto and Gatimaan. IRCTC says FT booking is not allowed in Suvidha trains.
  • The fare is 1.5 times the normal fare. A child without a berth pays half the adult base fare, which on this quota is 1.5 times the normal base fare.
  • IRCTC charges a service charge of ₹200 + GST per ticket.
  • Payment is only by international debit or credit card.
  • No concession is allowed.

Passport and visa on the train

The passport number and nationality of every passenger must be entered at booking. Each passenger must carry the original passport given at booking, with a valid visa. IRCTC says a passenger who cannot show them is treated as travelling without a ticket and charged a penalty of three times the fare.

Cancellation

When an FT quota ticket is cancelled, a flat 50% of the fare is deducted in addition to the normal cancellation charges, up to 4 hours before the scheduled departure and through the website only. Waitlisted and RAC FT tickets follow the General quota cancellation rules.

Not allowed on FT quota tickets

  • Change of name
  • Booking by agents
  • I-tickets
  • Travel insurance
  • VIKALP

The boarding point can be changed, and the ERS printed, only after a berth has been allotted.

In short

  • Up to 365 days ahead, 1.5 times fare, ₹200 + GST per ticket.
  • Carry the original passport and visa for every passenger.
  • 50% of the fare is deducted on cancellation, plus normal charges.
