IRCTC account rules: one ID, the passenger list and deactivation

Most IRCTC account problems come from a few rules in its Terms and Conditions: one ID per person, a short passenger list and no automation. Here is what they say.

Last checked: 24 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

One user ID per email and mobile number

IRCTC says no user can register more than once on the site, and only one user registration is allowed on each unique email ID and mobile number. More than one user ID on an email ID and mobile number may lead to deactivation of the ID. If a user registers more than one ID or books tickets on such multiple IDs, IRCTC reserves the right to deactivate all of those registrations and cancel any or all tickets booked on them without notice.

The master passenger list

IRCTC advises users to keep a maximum of six passengers in their Master Passengers List. If you keep more than six, the Master List becomes inaccessible to you. You can still book for other passengers by typing their details directly on the Passenger Reservation page.

What can get an ID deactivated

  • Registering more than once, or booking on several IDs.
  • Incomplete or incorrect registration details, or junk values.
  • An address that was entered incorrectly at registration.
  • Suspicious booking activity, including scripting tools or Tatkal software. IRCTC may also release the tickets booked on such IDs, and no refund of fare is admissible in cases of misuse.
  • Payment that the payment gateway does not authorise more than once for tickets you booked, which is liable to result in deregistration without notice.

IRCTC says its website and apps are for personal and non-commercial use, and that automation or scripting software is strictly prohibited.

Keep your login safe

You are responsible for keeping your password and account confidential, and for all activity under it. IRCTC asks you to log out at the end of each session and to report unauthorised use of your password or account straight away at equery.irctc.co.in. IRCTC may also ask you from time to time to provide and verify your email ID and mobile number. You confirm when registering that you are 18 or above. IRCTC says it is not responsible for financial loss from misuse of your ID, password or card details.

Foreign users

People from countries other than India, and NRIs, can register as Foreign Users by paying a registration fee. They can then book under the Foreign Tourist quota and the General quota. See our foreign tourist quota guide.

Who to contact

IRCTC's customer care number is 14646, open 24 hours. For general information, IRCTC lists equery.irctc.co.in.

In short

  • One user ID per email ID and mobile number.
  • Keep at most six people in the master list; type any others on the booking page.
  • Never use scripts or tools to book, and report misuse of your account at once.

Sources

  • IRCTC: Terms and Conditions
