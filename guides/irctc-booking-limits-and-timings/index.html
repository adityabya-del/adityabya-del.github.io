IRCTC booking limits and timings: tickets per month, passengers and hours

IRCTC limits how many tickets one user ID can book, how many passengers fit on one ticket and when booking is open. Here are the current numbers from IRCTC and the Ministry of Railways.

Last checked: 27 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

Tickets per month

User IDLimit per month
Not Aadhaar-verified12 tickets
Aadhaar-verified, with at least one passenger on the ticket Aadhaar-verified24 tickets

These limits were raised from 6 and 12 by the Ministry of Railways in June 2022. IRCTC's guide says that to book more than 12 tickets in a month, the user must complete Aadhaar KYC in the profile and add at least one Aadhaar-verified passenger. IRCTC also says an Aadhaar-authenticated user can book Tatkal and Premium Tatkal tickets; see our Aadhaar guide.

Passengers per ticket

  • A normal booking: up to six berths or seats at a time for one journey.
  • A Tatkal e-ticket: up to four passengers per PNR.

Booking hours

  • IRCTC's Terms and Conditions say internet booking is allowed from 00:20 to 23:45 IST every day, including Sundays, and that the hours can change without notice.
  • On the opening day (60 days ahead, not counting the journey date), booking opens only after 8 AM. Some intercity day trains have a shorter booking window. See our 60-day guide.
  • Tatkal opens at 10:00 for AC classes and 11:00 for non-AC classes, one day before the train leaves its starting station.

Checks while you book

  • Press Information Bureau releases say individual users are logged out if their enquiries exceed 25 per session during the opening hours of 08:00 to 12:00.
  • Checks are applied on the minimum time needed to enter the passenger details and the CAPTCHA, and a ticket cannot be booked before 35 seconds.

One account per person

  • Only one user registration is allowed on each email ID and mobile number. IRCTC says more than one user ID on the same email or mobile may lead to deactivation.
  • IRCTC advises keeping at most six passengers in the Master Passenger List; more than six makes the list inaccessible, though you can still type passengers in directly.
  • Automation and scripting software are prohibited. IRCTC can deactivate such users and release tickets booked through them without refund.

Convenience fee

IRCTC says its convenience fee on a successful e-ticket booking is not refundable under any circumstances.

In short

  • 12 tickets a month, or 24 with Aadhaar verification.
  • Up to 6 passengers per booking, 4 on Tatkal.
  • Booking open 00:20 to 23:45; opening day after 8 AM.

Sources

  • PIB, 6 June 2022: limit of online booking raised to 12 and 24 tickets a month
  • IRCTC: Book up to 24 tickets in a month by linking Aadhaar

  • PIB: Various measures for fast, safe and transparent e-ticketing
  • PIB: Opening of reservation centres round the clock
