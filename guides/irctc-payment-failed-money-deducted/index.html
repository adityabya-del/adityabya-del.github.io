IRCTC payment failed but money was deducted: what to check

If the page froze after you paid, do not pay again straight away. IRCTC's Terms describe the checks to make first and what happens to the money if no ticket was issued.

Last checked: 27 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

First, check whether the ticket was booked

IRCTC says that after a successful payment a Ticket Confirmation Page shows your PNR details. If that page does not appear because of a power failure, an internet failure or any other reason, open Booked Ticket History under My Account, then My Transactions. That page lists every ticket booked by you. If your reservation is there, note the transaction ID, the PNR and the other details. IRCTC also advises you to check your email.

If no ticket was issued

IRCTC says that if the reservation does not materialise for any reason, the entire amount debited from your card, bank or wallet account is credited back to your source account. If you want to try for the same reservation again, it is treated as a fresh booking.

Booking is subject to the fare and the convenience fee (including GST) being received from the bank through the payment gateway. IRCTC says that if one payment gateway is not working, you can try the other one.

What the Ministry of Railways says about timing

A Ministry of Railways release says that if money is debited and the ticket is not booked, IRCTC starts an automatic refund on the following day, and the refund is advised to the payment gateway or bank. It then takes 3 to 4 business days for net banking, wallet or cash card payments, and 6 to 7 business days for credit or debit card payments.

Do not pay twice for the same ticket

IRCTC says that if a user does not check Booked Tickets, makes a repeated transaction and also fails to cancel the ticket, the user has no right to claim the amount, and the loss is not borne by IRCTC or by the banks and payment gateways. If you ended up with two tickets for the same journey, cancel the extra one before the chart is prepared. See our cancellation guide.

How long does a refund take?

IRCTC says its payment reconciliation team works round the clock, but it gives no guarantee about how accurate or timely refunds reaching your card or bank account will be. It gives the reason as the many organisations involved in processing online payments, internet problems and bank holidays. IRCTC's own pages give no number of days; the Ministry of Railways release below does.

If money is still missing

  • Call IRCTC customer care on 14646, open 24 hours.
  • For general information, IRCTC lists equery.irctc.co.in.
  • For an e-ticket you could not cancel online, IRCTC says to email the e-ticket details to etickets@irctc.co.in.

Two warnings from the Terms

  • Payment that the gateway does not authorise more than once for tickets booked by you can lead to deregistration of your account without notice.
  • The IRCTC convenience fee collected on a successful booking is non-refundable under any circumstances.

In short

  • Open Booked Ticket History before you pay again.
  • If no ticket was issued, the full amount goes back to the source account.
  • IRCTC gives no guaranteed refund time; call 14646 if it does not arrive.

Sources
