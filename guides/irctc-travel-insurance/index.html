IRCTC travel insurance: 45 paise cover and what it pays

When you book an e-ticket on IRCTC you can add optional travel insurance for a few paise. Here is what IRCTC's terms say it covers.

Last checked: 19 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

Premium and who can take it

  • The premium is 45 paise per passenger, including all taxes, from 17 February 2024.
  • It is optional, but if you choose it, it applies to every passenger on the PNR.
  • It is offered only on confirmed, RAC and partly confirmed tickets, at the time of booking.
  • It is only for Indian citizens booking e-tickets on IRCTC. Foreign citizens are not eligible, and it is not available on Foreign Tourist quota tickets.

What it pays

IRCTC says the cover is the same for all classes:

EventSum insured
Death₹10,00,000
Permanent total disability₹10,00,000
Permanent partial disabilityUp to ₹7,50,000
Hospitalisation expenses for injury₹2,00,000
Transportation of mortal remains₹10,000

Hospitalisation cover is in addition to the death or disability cover.

What it covers

The cover is for a train accident or an "untoward incident" as defined in the Railways Act, 1989 (for example a violent attack, robbery or the accidental falling of a passenger from a train), from the train's actual departure to its actual arrival, including getting on and off. It also covers:

  • Road transport arranged by the railway if the train is terminated short of the destination
  • The diverted route if the train is diverted
  • The alternative train under VIKALP

Children

Children below 5 booked without a berth or seat are not covered. Children aged 5 to 11 are covered, with or without a berth.

Nominee and claims

The insurance company sends the policy details by SMS and email, with a link to fill in nomination details; the policy number is also shown in IRCTC's booked ticket history. If no nominee is filled in, a claim is settled with the legal heirs. IRCTC says the contract is between the passenger and the insurance company, and all correspondence and claims go directly to the insurer.

In short

  • 45 paise per passenger, for confirmed, RAC and partly confirmed e-tickets.
  • Up to ₹10 lakh for death or permanent total disability.
  • Fill in the nominee on the insurer's site after booking.
