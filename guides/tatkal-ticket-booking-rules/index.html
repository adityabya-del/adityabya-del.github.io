Tatkal ticket booking: timings, charges and rules

Tatkal is the railway's quota for last-minute travel. It opens the day before the train leaves, costs more than a normal ticket and has its own rules on who can book, which classes are covered and what you get back if you cancel. This guide explains each rule from IRCTC's own Tatkal FAQ and the Ministry of Railways, with worked examples so you know exactly what to expect before the booking window opens.

Last checked: 19 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or cancel.

When does Tatkal open?

IRCTC's Tatkal FAQ says a Tatkal e-ticket can be booked one day in advance, not counting the journey date, and that the day is counted from the train's originating station. On that opening day, the AC classes (2A, 3A, CC, EC and 3E) open at 10:00 and the non-AC classes (SL, FC and 2S) open at 11:00.

IRCTC gives this example: if a train leaves its originating station on 2 August, Tatkal booking opens at 10:00 on 1 August for AC classes and at 11:00 on 1 August for non-AC classes.

Boarding at an intermediate station? The opening day is worked out from the date the train starts its journey, not the date it reaches your station. A train that starts at 22:00 on 2 August and reaches your station at 04:00 on 3 August still opens for Tatkal on 1 August. If you count from 3 August, you will arrive at the booking screen a day late.

Which classes have Tatkal?

IRCTC states that Tatkal is allowed in all classes except First AC and Executive Class. In practice, that covers:

What does Tatkal cost?

The Tatkal charge is paid per passenger, on top of the normal ticket. It is 10% of the basic fare for second class and 30% of the basic fare for every other class, but it can never go below a minimum or above a maximum set for each class:

For a family of four in Sleeper with a basic fare of ₹500 each, the Tatkal charge alone is 4 × ₹150 = ₹600, because it is charged per passenger.

IRCTC also says that no concession is allowed on a Tatkal booking, so senior citizen and other concessions do not reduce a Tatkal ticket.

Can you book Tatkal from any station?

Yes, within the train's rules. IRCTC says Tatkal tickets are issued for the actual distance you travel, not only end to end, subject to the distance restriction that applies to the train. It also says the same Tatkal berth can be booked in several legs until the chart is prepared, so one berth can carry different passengers on different parts of the route.

Can you combine Tatkal with Ladies or General quota?

No. IRCTC's FAQ says Ladies quota and General quota cannot be chosen along with Tatkal. A Tatkal booking is a Tatkal-quota booking only. Our guide to lower berths and the Ladies quota explains how those quotas work on a normal booking.

Who can book Tatkal online?

A Ministry of Railways release dated 11 June 2025 changed who can book. From 1 July 2025, Tatkal tickets on IRCTC's website and app are available only to users whose accounts are authenticated with Aadhaar. From 15 July 2025, an Aadhaar-based OTP is also needed for online Tatkal bookings.

The same release covers bookings made elsewhere. At reservation counters and with authorised agents, Tatkal bookings from 15 July 2025 need an OTP sent to the mobile number given at the time of booking. Authorised agents may not book opening-day Tatkal tickets in the first 30 minutes of the window: 10:00 to 10:30 for AC classes and 11:00 to 11:30 for non-AC classes. Read more in our guide to Aadhaar and train ticket booking.

Before the window opens: a checklist

Everything on this list comes from the rules above.

  1. Work out the opening day from the date the train leaves its originating station, then subtract one day.
  2. Know your opening time: 10:00 for 2A, 3A, 3E and CC; 11:00 for SL and 2S.
  3. Check your IRCTC account is Aadhaar-authenticated, and keep the phone that receives the Aadhaar OTP with you.
  4. Check that Tatkal is offered for your train, class and stations. It is not available on every train or in every class.
  5. Expect no concession and plan for the Tatkal charge per passenger on top of the normal fare.
  6. Be sure before you pay: a confirmed Tatkal ticket is not refunded if you cancel it.

If you cancel a Tatkal ticket

IRCTC says no refund is given when a confirmed Tatkal ticket is cancelled. For a waitlisted Tatkal ticket, or a contingent cancellation, charges are deducted under the existing railway refund rules. If your train is cancelled, runs very late or is diverted, the refund rules in our guide to train cancellations, delays and TDR apply instead. For step-by-step cancellation, see how to cancel an IRCTC e-ticket.

Questions people ask

Is there Tatkal in First AC?

No. IRCTC allows Tatkal in all classes except First AC and Executive Class.

Do senior citizens get a concession on Tatkal?

No. IRCTC says no concession is allowed in a Tatkal booking.

My Tatkal ticket is waitlisted. Can I cancel it?

Yes. A waitlisted Tatkal ticket can be cancelled, and charges are deducted under the existing railway rules. Only a confirmed Tatkal ticket gets no refund.

How do I contact IRCTC about a Tatkal booking?

IRCTC's FAQ gives care@irctc.co.in and its 24-hour customer support numbers 0755-3934141 and 0755-6610661.

Checking seats before Tatkal opens

In short

  • Tatkal opens one day before the train leaves its originating station: 10:00 for AC classes, 11:00 for non-AC.
  • All classes except First AC and Executive Class.
  • The charge is 10% (second class) or 30% (other classes) of the basic fare, kept within a minimum and maximum per class, and charged per passenger.
  • No concessions, and no Ladies or General quota alongside Tatkal.
  • Online booking needs an Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC account and an Aadhaar OTP.
  • No refund on a confirmed Tatkal ticket.
