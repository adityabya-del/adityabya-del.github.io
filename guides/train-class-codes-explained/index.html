Train class codes: SL, 3A, 2A, 1A, CC, EC, 3E and 2S

Your ticket shows a short class code. This guide lists the codes used on IRCTC and Indian Railways pages and the full name each page gives.

Last checked: 24 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

AC and non-AC classes

IRCTC's Terms group the classes this way for Tatkal booking: the AC classes are 2A, 3A, CC, EC and 3E, and the non-AC classes are SL, FC and 2S. First Class Air-conditioned is written 1A in the same Terms.

Some official pages write the AC classes as 2AC and 3AC and others as 2A and 3A. They are the same classes.

Things the class decides

  • Cancellation charges. The flat minimum is set by class. IRCTC lists ₹240 for AC First Class/Executive Class, ₹200 for AC 2 Tier/First Class, ₹180 for AC 3 Tier/AC Chair Car/AC 3 Economy, ₹120 for Sleeper and ₹60 for Second Class. See our cancellation guide.
  • Tatkal. IRCTC says Tatkal berths are earmarked in all important trains in different classes except 1A. AC Tatkal opens at 10:00 and non-AC Tatkal at 11:00 on the day before departure. See our Tatkal guide.
  • Ladies quota. IRCTC says it is generally earmarked in Sleeper (SL) and Second Sitting (2S).
  • Lower berths. The Ministry of Railways says lower berths are earmarked for senior citizens, women of 45 years and above and pregnant women in Sleeper, 3AC and 2AC. See our lower berth guide.
  • Seats for persons with disabilities. Four berths in Sleeper and in 3AC/3E, and four seats in 2S or CC on trains with more than two coaches of that class.

In short

  • AC classes: 1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC. Non-AC: SL, FC, 2S.
  • The class sets the cancellation charge and whether Tatkal is available.
