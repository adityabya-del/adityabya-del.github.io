How to complain about a train journey: RailMadad, 139 and IRCTC

Complaints about the train and complaints about an IRCTC booking go to different places. This guide lists the official channels.

Last checked: 27 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

Journey problems: RailMadad

The Ministry of Railways describes RailMadad as Indian Railways' grievance redressal system, an integrated platform for grievances, assistance and enquiry (PIB, 25 March 2026). You can reach it through:

  • Helpline 139
  • The RailMadad website
  • The RailMadad app
  • SMS

RailMadad also lets passengers give feedback on how their complaint was resolved.

What helpline 139 offers

  • 139 is available round the clock in 12 languages, and it replaced more than 15 earlier helplines. The security helpline 182 was merged into 139 from 1 April 2021.
  • Menu of 139: press 1 for security and medical assistance, which connects straight to a call centre executive; press 2 for enquiries (PNR status, train running, fares, wheelchair booking, meal booking and more); press 3 for catering complaints; press 4 for general complaints; press 5 for bribery and corruption; press 9 for the status of your complaint.
  • Passengers without a smartphone can call 139 and talk to an executive, who helps lodge the grievance.

Emergencies on the train

The Ministry says passengers needing immediate help can complain on the RailMadad portal or call helpline 139, which is integrated with the Emergency Response Support System number 112.

Booking problems: IRCTC

IRCTC's Terms and Conditions list these channels for railway tickets booked on IRCTC:

  • Customer care: 14646, 24x7, in Hindi, English, Punjabi, Bengali, Assamese, Odia, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam
  • From outside India: +91-8044647999 or +91-8035734999
  • General queries: equery.irctc.co.in
  • E-ticket cancellation problems: etickets@irctc.co.in

IRCTC also says you should check your ticket for errors as soon as you get it and report any error straight away on 14646.

Food on the train

Indian Railways' catering page says complaint books are available with the TTE, the pantry car manager and the station manager. The Ministry's March 2026 release lists steps against overcharging, including an SMS to passengers with a link to the menu and tariff, and POS machines for billing.

In short

  • Train journey: RailMadad, or call 139.
  • IRCTC booking: 14646 or equery.irctc.co.in.
  • Food: ask for the complaint book.
