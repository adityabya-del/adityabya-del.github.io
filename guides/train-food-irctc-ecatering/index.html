Food on a train: IRCTC eCatering and how to complain

IRCTC runs an official service for ordering food to your seat at a station on your route. This guide covers how it works and where to send a complaint about food.

Last checked: 24 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

Ordering with IRCTC eCatering

IRCTC eCatering says you enter your PNR, view the restaurants and food available for your journey, place the order and pay online or pay on delivery, and the food is delivered at the station you choose. You can order on the eCatering website or on its Food on Track app.

IRCTC eCatering says it delivers at over 300 railway stations. Check the station list on its site for your route.

Other ways to order

  • Call 1323.
  • WhatsApp: IRCTC eCatering says it has a dedicated business WhatsApp number, +91-8750001323, for orders.
  • Groups of 15 or more can ask for its group order service.

IRCTC eCatering warns about unauthorised suppliers who claim to be linked to it. Use the official website or the Food on Track app.

If your ticket is cancelled

IRCTC eCatering lists automatic cancellation of an order if the PNR gets cancelled as one of the benefits of ordering through it.

Meals served by the railway

Indian Railways publishes catering charges for Rajdhani, Shatabdi and Duronto Express trains, with a revised tariff for tea or coffee, breakfast and standard meals. Check the tariff on the Indian Railways catering charges page, as the figures are shown there.

IRCTC lists these menu rates for standard items, one rate for food bought at a station and one for food served in the train.

The Press Information Bureau (15 November 2019) also lists a Janta meal at ₹20, a snack meal at ₹50 and biryani at ₹80 (vegetarian), ₹90 (egg) and ₹110 (chicken). Catering on Rajdhani, Shatabdi and Duronto Express trains is included in the ticket fare, so there is no separate rate for it. Rates can change, so confirm on the IRCTC menu rate pages or the ordering screen before you pay.

How to complain about food

  • Call the toll-free number 1800111321.
  • Ask for the complaint book, which Indian Railways says is available with the TTE, the Pantry Car Manager and the Station Manager.
  • For any other complaint, Indian Railways gives its customer care site, customercare.indianrailways.gov.in.

For a wider complaint such as cleanliness or staff, see our RailMadad and 139 guide.

In short

  • Use the official IRCTC eCatering site or app with your PNR.
  • The order cancels automatically if the PNR is cancelled.
  • Food complaints: 1800111321 or the complaint book.
