Train luggage allowance by class: how much you can carry free

Every passenger can carry some luggage in the coach for free. The amount depends on the class. This guide sets out the limits from Indian Railways' luggage rules.

Last checked: 19 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

Allowance by class

Indian Railways' luggage rules give each passenger a free allowance, a small marginal allowance on top, and a maximum that can be carried in the compartment (the maximum includes the free allowance):

ClassFree allowanceMarginal allowanceMaximum in the coach
AC First Class70 kg15 kg150 kg
AC 2-tier / First Class50 kg10 kg100 kg
AC 3-tier / AC Chair Car40 kg10 kg40 kg
Sleeper40 kg10 kg80 kg
Second Class35 kg10 kg70 kg

Children aged 5 and below 12 get half the free allowance, up to 50 kg.

Carrying more than the free allowance

  • You can carry excess luggage in the compartment up to the maximum for your class if you book it at the luggage office and pay 1.5 times the luggage rate. The rules ask you to get your ticket endorsed with the luggage ticket details before the journey.
  • Luggage beyond the maximum can be booked in advance and carried in the brake van.
  • Luggage that must go on the same train as you should be presented at the luggage office of the booking station at least 30 minutes before departure.
  • The minimum charge for luggage is ₹30.

If extra luggage is found unbooked

  • Above the free allowance but within the marginal allowance: charged at 1.5 times the luggage rate.
  • Above the marginal allowance, or above the maximum: the weight above the free allowance is charged at six times the luggage rate, with a minimum of ₹50.

Suitcase size

Trunks, suitcases and boxes up to 100 cm × 60 cm × 25 cm (length × breadth × height) may be carried in the compartment. Anything larger in any one dimension has to be booked and carried in the brake van. In AC 3-tier and AC Chair Car, the maximum suitcase size is 55 cm × 45 cm × 22.5 cm.

What cannot be carried

The rules say offensive, explosive, dangerous and inflammable articles, empty gas cylinders, dead poultry and game, acids and other corrosive substances are not accepted as luggage. Merchandise is not allowed as personal luggage in the compartment. Scooters, cycles and similar articles get no free allowance.

Medical oxygen

An oxygen cylinder with its stand, for a patient travelling under a medical certificate, may be carried in all classes, and the free allowance applies to it.

The Indian Railways luggage page shows no date, so the date these limits took effect cannot be confirmed from it.

In short

  • Free: 70 kg (1A), 50 kg (2A), 40 kg (3A, CC, SL), 35 kg (second class).
  • Book extra weight at the luggage office; unbooked excess can cost six times the rate.
  • Suitcases up to 100 × 60 × 25 cm in the coach; 55 × 45 × 22.5 cm in 3A and CC.
