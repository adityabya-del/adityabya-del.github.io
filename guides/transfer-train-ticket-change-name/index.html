Can you transfer a train ticket or change the passenger name?

A reserved train ticket is not transferable as a rule, but Railway rules allow a name change in a few cases. This guide sets out who qualifies and how.

Last checked: 19 November 2026. Railway rules change, so confirm on IRCTC before you book or travel.

The basic rule

IRCTC says accommodation booked on an e-ticket is not transferable. It is valid only if one of the passengers booked on it shows one of the accepted ID cards in original during the journey (see our ID proof guide).

When the name can be changed

IRCTC lists two cases, for a passenger holding a confirmed e-reservation:

  • To a family member. The passenger makes a written request at the nearest Railway Reservation Office at least 24 hours before the train's scheduled departure. The ticket can go to a father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband or wife.
  • Government servants on duty. The appropriate authority makes the written request at least 24 hours before the scheduled departure.

IRCTC says such a request is granted once only.

What to take to the counter

  • The printout of the Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS)
  • Original photo identity proof of one of the passengers on the ticket
  • Proof of the blood relation with the person who will travel

The name change is done at a reservation office, not online.

Tickets where the name cannot be changed

  • A ticket booked under current booking: IRCTC does not allow any name, age or gender change.
  • A Foreign Tourist quota ticket: IRCTC says no change of name is allowed under any circumstances.
  • A Tatkal ticket: IRCTC's Tatkal page says modification of a Tatkal ticket in any form is not permitted.

Changing the date or train

IRCTC says changing the journey date or train of an e-ticket is not available. Its FAQ says to cancel the original ticket online and book a fresh one. Cancellation charges apply; see our cancellation guide.

In short

  • Not transferable, except to listed family members or for government servants on duty.
  • Written request at a reservation office, at least 24 hours before departure, once only.
  • For a new date or train: cancel and rebook.
