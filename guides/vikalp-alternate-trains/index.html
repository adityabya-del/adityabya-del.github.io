IRCTC VIKALP: moving a waitlisted ticket to another train

VIKALP lets a waitlisted passenger ask IRCTC to try other trains if the original one does not confirm. This guide sets out the rules IRCTC publishes.

The short answer

VIKALP is an IRCTC option for waitlisted passengers. You pick up to seven alternate trains, and if you are still fully waitlisted after charting, IRCTC may move the whole booking to one of them. It does not guarantee a confirmed berth.

Who can use it

Waitlisted passengers on any train type and class, whatever the booking quota or concession. Opting in does not guarantee a confirmed berth, which depends on train and berth availability.

How the alternate train is chosen

  • You can select up to seven alternate trains.
  • The alternate train must depart between 30 minutes and 72 hours after the original train’s scheduled departure.
  • Passengers who remain fully waitlisted after charting are considered for allotment.
  • You can also opt in later, before charting, through the booked ticket history link.

What happens to the original ticket

  • All passengers on a PNR are moved together, or none are, and the class stays the same.
  • Boarding or destination stations may shift to nearby cluster stations.
  • Transferred passengers appear on a separate list, not the original waitlist charts.
  • If you are allotted an alternate berth, you cannot board the original train.
  • Journey changes are not allowed after allotment. You would need to cancel and rebook.

Charges

No extra charge applies, and there is no refund for any fare difference, including Tatkal charges. Cancellation charges follow the berth and train status on the alternate train. A passenger who cancels after allotment is treated as confirmed, so the standard cancellation rules apply. If you do not travel on the alternate train, you can claim a refund by filing a TDR.

Check your PNR twice

IRCTC advises checking PNR status after charting, and again after the alternate train’s chart is prepared, because last-minute changes can happen. Once a train list is selected it can be updated only once, and the scheme cannot be changed after you opt in.

A note on dates

The IRCTC document shows no date. Check IRCTC for the current terms before you opt in.
