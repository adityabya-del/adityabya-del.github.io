What happens to a waitlisted e-ticket after the chart is prepared

The chart is the deadline for a waitlisted ticket. Here is what IRCTC says happens at that point.

The short answer

If every passenger on an e-ticket is still waitlisted after the chart is prepared, IRCTC cancels the ticket, the names are dropped from the chart and the passengers are not allowed to board.

What IRCTC says happens, by ticket status

Status after the chart is preparedWhat happens
Fully confirmed, or fully RACThe passenger’s name appears in the chart and they can travel.
Partly confirmed or partly RAC, with the rest waitlistedThe names appear in the charts, including the waitlisted passengers.
Fully waitlisted (every passenger in the booking)The names are dropped from the charts and IRCTC cancels the ticket.

If your ticket is cancelled

  • The passenger is “not allowed to board the train”. A person found travelling is treated as a passenger travelling without a ticket under Railway Rules.
  • IRCTC arranges the refund from the Railways and credits it electronically to the customer’s account (or the agent’s account for an agent booking).

What you can do before the chart

Check your PNR status often as the journey date gets close. ClearBerth’s PNR status page shows it and the app can alert you when the waitlist number changes. IRCTC also offers an option to move a waitlisted ticket to another train, described in our VIKALP guide. To cancel a ticket yourself, see how IRCTC e-ticket cancellation works.

A note on dates

The IRCTC page this guide is based on carries a 2019 copyright line and no other date. IRCTC may have changed its rules since, so check IRCTC before relying on it.
