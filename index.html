Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you download.

Is ClearBerth free?

Yes, to start. PNR status, the berth diagram and live status are free and supported by ads. If you want alerts, you pay once per journey. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

Is it the official IRCTC app?

No. ClearBerth is an independent app made for travellers. Use it alongside IRCTC for the things only IRCTC does.

Where does the train data come from?

From railway data providers. Live running data can lag or change, so treat times as best estimates and always follow station announcements for anything critical.

Which coach types does the seat diagram cover?

Sleeper, AC sleeper classes, and sitting classes such as Chair Car and Second Sitting. Each shows the layout that class actually has.

Can I book tickets in ClearBerth?

Yes. The Book tab lets you search seat availability and book. After you book, your PNR is ready to track in the same app.

Which languages does it support?

English and Hindi, including alerts.

Where can I download it?

ClearBerth is available on the App Store and Google Play in India.
