Your train, live on your lock screen

Turn on a Live Journey Alert in ClearBerth and your train sits on your phone's lock screen for the whole journey. You see where it is and how late it is without opening an app or refreshing a page.

What you see on your lock screen

  • Your train number and PNR.
  • Your boarding station and your destination, each with its time.
  • Whether the train is on time or late.
  • A small train moving along a track as the journey goes on.
  • How many km are left to your destination.
  • Your platform number, when it is known.

On iPhone this is a Live Activity, so it also shows in the Dynamic Island. On Android it is a live card on your lock screen and in your notifications.

Alerts you get, without asking

  • Boarding: when your train is about 1 hour away from your station, and again at 30 minutes.
  • Journey started: when the train leaves your station, with the delay if it is running late.
  • Arrival: when your train is about 1 hour, 30 minutes and 15 minutes from your destination.
  • Arrived: when the train reaches your destination.
  • Platform changed: if your train's platform changes.
  • If the train is diverted or cancelled, you get a push alert too.

Alerts come in English or Hindi. In the last hour before you board, and the last hour before you arrive, the card refreshes every 5 minutes.

Why it is easier than refreshing

Most train apps make you open the app, type the train number and check again every few minutes. With ClearBerth you set the alert once, lock your phone and put it away. The train comes to you.

What it costs

A Live Journey Alert is ₹29 for one journey. You pay once in the app. There is no subscription. It also removes ads while you check that journey's train.

How to turn it on

  1. Open ClearBerth and go to the Tracking tab.
  2. Enter your train number and the date you board.
  3. Get the Live Journey Alert for ₹29.
  4. Allow notifications when your phone asks.

Questions

Does ClearBerth show my train on the lock screen?

Yes. With a Live Journey Alert, your train shows on the lock screen for the journey: an iPhone Live Activity (also in the Dynamic Island) or a live card on Android.

Do I need to keep the app open?

No. Set the alert once and lock your phone. The lock-screen card and the alerts arrive on their own.

How much does it cost?

₹29 per journey, paid once in the app. There is no subscription.

Do the alerts come in Hindi?

Yes. ClearBerth's alerts come in English or Hindi.
