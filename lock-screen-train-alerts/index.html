Your PNR status on your lock screen

Every time your PNR status changes, ClearBerth sends a PNR Update notification to your lock screen. When your ticket is confirmed, it shows your coach and berth.

When ClearBerth checks your PNR

Time left before your train leavesClearBerth checks your PNR
The moment you turn on the alertOnce, straight away. You get your current status.
More than 7 daysEvery 2 days, at 6 PM
7 days or lessEvery day, at 6 PM
Last 24 hoursEvery 2 hours
Last 24 hours, in the hours around chart preparationEvery 30 minutes
After that, until departureEvery hour
About 90 to 30 minutes before departure (final chart)Every 30 minutes

You get a notification only when your status changes; if nothing changed at a check, no message is sent. Checks stop when every passenger on the ticket is confirmed, or when the train leaves.

Turn it on

  1. Open ClearBerth and tap PNR.
  2. Enter your 10-digit PNR and tap Check status.
  3. Your current status shows (in this example, RLWL 8). Select the date of journey, as printed on your ticket or SMS.
  4. Tap Get PNR Status Alerts (₹29).

Turn on the live train card in 3 steps

  1. Open ClearBerth and tap Tracking.
  2. Enter your train number and the day you board.
  3. Get the Live Journey Alert for ₹29.
