Odisha

Odisha’s coastal rail line connects Bhubaneswar and Puri with Kolkata, Chennai and Delhi. The Sun Temple at Konark, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, has no station of its own; Puri, roughly 35 km away, is the nearest railhead.
