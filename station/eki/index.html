West Bengal

New Jalpaiguri, near Siliguri in West Bengal, is the main railhead for the Darjeeling hills, Sikkim and the North East. The narrow-gauge Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, runs from New Jalpaiguri up to Darjeeling.
