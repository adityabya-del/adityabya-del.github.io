Tatkal for 11040

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Maharashtra Express starts from Gondia Jn at 08:10, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Gondia Jn. Maharashtra Express reaches Daund Chord Line on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Daund Chord Line, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
