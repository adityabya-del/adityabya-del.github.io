Tatkal for 11077

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Jhelum Express starts from Pune Jn at 17:20, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Pune Jn. Jhelum Express reaches Chalisgaon Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Chalisgaon Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
