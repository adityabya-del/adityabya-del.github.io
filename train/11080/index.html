Tatkal for 11080

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Mumbai Ltt Express starts from Gorakhpur Jn at 05:05, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Gorakhpur Jn. Mumbai Ltt Express reaches Rani Kamalapati on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Rani Kamalapati, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
