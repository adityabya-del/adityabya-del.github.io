Tatkal for 11303

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Solapur Weekly Express starts from Mumbai Ltt at 12:50, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Mumbai Ltt. Solapur Weekly Express reaches Latur Road on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Latur Road, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
