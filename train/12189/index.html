Tatkal for 12189

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Mahakoshal Express starts from Jabalpur at 18:10, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Jabalpur. Mahakoshal Express reaches Mahoba on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Mahoba, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
