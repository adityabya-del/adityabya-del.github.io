Tatkal for 12253

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Anga Express starts from Smvt Bengaluru at 13:50, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Smvt Bengaluru. Anga Express reaches Vijayawada Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Vijayawada Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
