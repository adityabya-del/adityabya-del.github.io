Tatkal for 12307

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Jodhpur Superfast Express starts from Howrah Jn at 23:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Howrah Jn. Jodhpur Superfast Express reaches Bardhaman on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Bardhaman, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
