Tatkal for 12322

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Howrah Mail starts from Mumbai Csmt at 22:15, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Mumbai Csmt. Howrah Mail reaches Kasara on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Kasara, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
