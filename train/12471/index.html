Tatkal for 12471

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Swaraj Express starts from Bandra Terminus at 11:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Bandra Terminus. Swaraj Express reaches Sawai Madhopur Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Sawai Madhopur Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
