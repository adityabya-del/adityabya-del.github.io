Tatkal for 12616

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Grand Trunk Express starts from New Delhi at 16:10, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from New Delhi. Grand Trunk Express reaches Bina Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Bina Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
