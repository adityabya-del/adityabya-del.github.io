Tatkal for 12627

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Karnataka Express starts from KSR Bengaluru at 19:20, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from KSR Bengaluru. Karnataka Express reaches Guntakal Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Guntakal Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
