Tatkal for 12650

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Karnataka Sampark Kranti Express starts from Hazrat Nizamuddin Jn at 08:20, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Hazrat Nizamuddin Jn. Karnataka Sampark Kranti Express reaches Balharshah on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Balharshah, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
