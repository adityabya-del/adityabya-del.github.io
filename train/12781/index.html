Tatkal for 12781

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Hazrat Nizamuddin Swarna Jayanti Sf Express starts from Mysore Jn at 19:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Mysore Jn. Hazrat Nizamuddin Swarna Jayanti Sf Express reaches Davangere on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Davangere, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
