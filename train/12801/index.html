Tatkal for 12801

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Purushottam Express starts from Puri at 21:55, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Puri. Purushottam Express reaches Jajpur K Road on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Jajpur K Road, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
