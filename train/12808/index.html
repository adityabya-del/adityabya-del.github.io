Tatkal for 12808

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Samata Express starts from Hazrat Nizamuddin Jn at 07:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Hazrat Nizamuddin Jn. Samata Express reaches Nagpur Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Nagpur Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
