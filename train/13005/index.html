Tatkal for 13005

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Amritsar Mail starts from Howrah Jn at 19:20, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Howrah Jn. Amritsar Mail reaches Jhajha on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Jhajha, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
