13025 at a glance

Bhopal Weekly Express covers 1456 km from Howrah Jn to Bhopal Jn in 29h 45m, an average speed of about 49 km/h including stops. It leaves Howrah Jn at 12:35 and reaches Bhopal Jn at 18:20 on day 2 of the journey, with 27 stops in between. The longest stretch without a halt is Bargawan to Beohari, 129 km. The longest scheduled halt is at Barka Kana, about 20 minutes. These are timetable figures. Real running times change with delays, so check train 13025 live in ClearBerth before you leave.

Tatkal for 13025

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Bhopal Weekly Express starts from Howrah Jn at 12:35, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Howrah Jn. Bhopal Weekly Express reaches Garhwa on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Garhwa, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
