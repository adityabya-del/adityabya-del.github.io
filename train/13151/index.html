Tatkal for 13151

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Jammu Tawi Express starts from Kolkatta Terminal at 11:45, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Kolkatta Terminal. Jammu Tawi Express reaches Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
