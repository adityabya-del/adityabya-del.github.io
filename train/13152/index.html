Tatkal for 13152

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Kolkata Express starts from Jammu Tawi at 20:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Jammu Tawi. Kolkata Express reaches Jalandhar Cantt on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Jalandhar Cantt, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
