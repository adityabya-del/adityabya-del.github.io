Tatkal for 13163

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Hate Bazare Express starts from Sealdah at 21:15, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Sealdah. Hate Bazare Express reaches Salar on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Salar, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
