Tatkal for 13248

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Kamakhya Capital Express starts from Ara at 21:05, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Ara. Kamakhya Capital Express reaches Mokama on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Mokama, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
