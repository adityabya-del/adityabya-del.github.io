Tatkal for 13401

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Danapur Intercity Express starts from Bhagalpur at 05:25, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.
