Tatkal for 14007

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Anand Vihar Terminal Sadbhavana Express starts from Raxaul Jn at 23:45, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Raxaul Jn. Anand Vihar Terminal Sadbhavana Express reaches Ghorasahan on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Ghorasahan, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
