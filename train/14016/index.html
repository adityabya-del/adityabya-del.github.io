Tatkal for 14016

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Raxaul Sadbhavana Express starts from Anand Vihar Terminal at 16:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Anand Vihar Terminal. Raxaul Sadbhavana Express reaches Hardoi on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Hardoi, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
