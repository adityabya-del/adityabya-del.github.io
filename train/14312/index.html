Tatkal for 14312

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Ala Hazrat Express starts from Bhuj at 16:15, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Bhuj. Ala Hazrat Express reaches Palanpur Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Palanpur Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
