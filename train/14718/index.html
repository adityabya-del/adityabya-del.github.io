Tatkal for 14718

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Bikaner Express starts from Haridwar Jn at 16:25, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Haridwar Jn. Bikaner Express reaches Bhiwani Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Bhiwani Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
