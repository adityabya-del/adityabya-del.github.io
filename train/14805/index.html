Tatkal for 14805

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Barmer AC Express starts from Yesvantpur Jn at 12:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Yesvantpur Jn. Barmer AC Express reaches Vijayapura on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Vijayapura, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
