15011 at a glance

Chandigarh Express covers 702 km from Lucknow Junction to Chandigarh in 15h 05m, an average speed of about 47 km/h including stops. It leaves Lucknow Junction at 23:55 and reaches Chandigarh at 15:00 on day 2 of the journey, with 18 stops in between. The longest stretch without a halt is Shahjahanpur Jn to Chandausi Jn, 136 km. Tatkal for 15011

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Chandigarh Express starts from Lucknow Junction at 23:55, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Lucknow Junction. Chandigarh Express reaches Hardoi on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Hardoi, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
