Tatkal for 15119

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Dehradun Janta Express starts from Banaras at 08:20, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Banaras. Dehradun Janta Express reaches Nagina on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Nagina, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
