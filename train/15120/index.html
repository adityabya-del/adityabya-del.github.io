15120 at a glance

Banaras Janta Express covers 848 km from Dehradoon to Banaras in 20h 35m, an average speed of about 41 km/h including stops. It leaves Dehradoon at 18:30 and reaches Banaras at 15:05 on day 2 of the journey, with 45 stops in between. The longest stretch without a halt is Bareilly (Nr) to Shahjahanpur Jn, 72 km. The longest scheduled halt is at Haridwar Jn, about 10 minutes. These are timetable figures. Real running times change with delays, so check train 15120 live in ClearBerth before you leave.

Tatkal for 15120

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Banaras Janta Express starts from Dehradoon at 18:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Dehradoon. Banaras Janta Express reaches Moradabad on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Moradabad, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
