Tatkal for 15280

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Poorabiya Express starts from Anand Vihar Terminal at 17:10, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Anand Vihar Terminal. Poorabiya Express reaches Lucknow Charbagh on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Lucknow Charbagh, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
