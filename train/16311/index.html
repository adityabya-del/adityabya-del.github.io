Tatkal for 16311

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Thiruvananthapuram North Express starts from Shri Ganganagar at 14:10, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Shri Ganganagar. Thiruvananthapuram North Express reaches Luni Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Luni Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
