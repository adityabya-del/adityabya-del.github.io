Tatkal for 16352

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Mumbai Ltt Express starts from Nagercoil Jn at 06:15, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Nagercoil Jn. Mumbai Ltt Express reaches Cuddapah on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Cuddapah, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
