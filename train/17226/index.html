Tatkal for 17226

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Narasapur Amaravati Express starts from Sss Hubballi Jn at 13:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Sss Hubballi Jn. Narasapur Amaravati Express reaches Kurichedu on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Kurichedu, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
