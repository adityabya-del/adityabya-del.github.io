Tatkal for 18029

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Shalimar Express starts from Mumbai Ltt at 22:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Mumbai Ltt. Shalimar Express reaches Igatpuri on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Igatpuri, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
