Tatkal for 18030

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Mumbai Ltt Express starts from Shalimar at 15:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Shalimar. Mumbai Ltt Express reaches Jharsuguda Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Jharsuguda Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
