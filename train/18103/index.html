Tatkal for 18103

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Amritsar Jallianwala Bagh Express starts from Tatanagar Jn at 20:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Tatanagar Jn. Amritsar Jallianwala Bagh Express reaches Bokaro Steel City on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Bokaro Steel City, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
