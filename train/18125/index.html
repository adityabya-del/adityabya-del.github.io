Tatkal for 18125

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Rourkela - Puri Express starts from Rourkela at 07:50, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Rourkela. Rourkela - Puri Express reaches Puri on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Puri, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
