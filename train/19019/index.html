Tatkal for 19019

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Haridwar Express starts from Bandra Terminus at 00:20, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Bandra Terminus. Haridwar Express reaches Mathura Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Mathura Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
