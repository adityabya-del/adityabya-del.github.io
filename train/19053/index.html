Tatkal for 19053

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Muzaffarpur Express starts from Surat at 07:35, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Surat. Muzaffarpur Express reaches Bina Malkheri Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Bina Malkheri Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
