Tatkal for 19616

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Udaipur City Kavi Guru Express starts from Kamakhya at 18:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Kamakhya. Udaipur City Kavi Guru Express reaches New Jalpaiguri on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from New Jalpaiguri, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
