Tatkal for 20635

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Ananthapuri Superfast Express starts from Chennai Egmore at 19:50, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Chennai Egmore. Ananthapuri Superfast Express reaches Tiruchchirappalli Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Tiruchchirappalli Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
