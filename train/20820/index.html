Tatkal for 20820

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Puri Dwarka Sf Express starts from Okha at 19:05, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Okha. Puri Dwarka Sf Express reaches Surendranagar on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Surendranagar, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
