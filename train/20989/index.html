Tatkal for 20989

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Chandigarh Sf Express starts from Udaipur City at 16:05, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Udaipur City. Chandigarh Sf Express reaches Dausa Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Dausa Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
