Tatkal for 22101

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Madurai Weekly Sf Express starts from Mumbai Ltt at 13:15, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Mumbai Ltt. Madurai Weekly Sf Express reaches Raichur on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Raichur, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
