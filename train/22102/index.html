Tatkal for 22102

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Mumbai Ltt Weekly Sf Express starts from Madurai Jn at 16:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Madurai Jn. Mumbai Ltt Weekly Sf Express reaches Tiruttani on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Tiruttani, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
