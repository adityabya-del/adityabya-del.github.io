Tatkal for 22603

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Villupuram Sf Express starts from Kharagpur Jn at 14:00, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Kharagpur Jn. Villupuram Sf Express reaches Vizianagram Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Vizianagram Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
