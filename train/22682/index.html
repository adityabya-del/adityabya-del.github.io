Tatkal for 22682

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Mysuru Weekly Sf Express starts from MGR Chennai Central at 23:30, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from MGR Chennai Central. Mysuru Weekly Sf Express reaches Arakkonam Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Arakkonam Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
