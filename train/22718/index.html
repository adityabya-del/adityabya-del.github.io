Tatkal for 22718

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Rajkot Sf Express starts from Secunderabad Jn at 15:10, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Secunderabad Jn. Rajkot Sf Express reaches Daund Jn on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Daund Jn, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
