Tatkal for 22850

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Shalimar Weekly Sf Express starts from Secunderabad Jn at 03:55, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Secunderabad Jn. Shalimar Weekly Sf Express reaches Bhadrakh on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Bhadrakh, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
