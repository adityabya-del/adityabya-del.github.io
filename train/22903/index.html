Tatkal for 22903

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Bhuj AC Sf Express starts from Bandra Terminus at 23:45, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Bandra Terminus. Bhuj AC Sf Express reaches Borivali on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Borivali, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
