Tatkal for 22945

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Saurashtra Mail starts from Mumbai Central at 21:05, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Mumbai Central. Saurashtra Mail reaches Surat on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Surat, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
