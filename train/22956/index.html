Tatkal for 22956

IRCTC opens Tatkal one day before the train leaves its originating station. Kutch Sf Express starts from Bhuj at 20:25, so Tatkal for a run opens the day before that date: at 10:00 for AC classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC) and at 11:00 for non-AC classes (SL, 2S). Whether this train has a Tatkal quota in your class and between your stations is shown on IRCTC when you book.

Boarding later on the route? The day still counts from Bhuj. Kutch Sf Express reaches Dhrangadhra on day 2 of its run, so for a ticket from Dhrangadhra, Tatkal opens 2 days before the date you board there.
